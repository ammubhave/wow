Your captain has set up a workspace for the hunt and sent you a link. This page gets you from that link to working on a puzzle with your team. It takes about five minutes.

## Join the workspace

<Steps>

1. **Open the invitation link.** It looks like `https://join.wafflehaus.io/your-team`. Your captain can also give you the workspace ID and password instead.
2. **Sign in or create an account.** If you're not signed in, WOW asks you to log in. New to WOW? Choose **Create an account**, then either **Sign up with Google**, or enter your name, email and a password and choose **Create account**. Afterwards WOW takes you straight back to the invitation.
3. **Enter the workspace password** and choose **Join**. Your captain will have shared the password with the link (or sent a link with the password in it, which skips this step).

</Steps>

You're now a member, and you land on the team's blackboard. The workspace also appears on **My workspaces** from now on.

<Callout type="tip">
  Once you have an account, you can add a passkey on your [Account page](/docs/reference/account)
  and next time sign in with **Continue with a passkey**.
</Callout>

If you only have the ID and password, go to **My workspaces** and fill in the **Join a workspace** card instead.

## Find a puzzle

The blackboard lists every round, and under each round its metas and the puzzles that feed them. A few things help you choose:

- **Status and importance**: puzzles marked **Stuck** or **Needs Eyes** want fresh eyes; ones marked **Important** are the team's priority.
- **Working on this**: avatars of who has each puzzle open right now. A puzzle with nobody on it may need you; one with five people probably doesn't.
- **Filters**: in the toolbar, **Hide solved** clears away finished puzzles, and **Tags** narrows to the kinds of puzzle you like.
- **Search**: press <Kbd>/</Kbd> and type. Search covers names, answers, notes and tags.

When you've picked one, click its name to open it.

## Work on it with your team

The puzzle page has everything for one puzzle in one place:

<Screenshot
  src={puzzlePageImage}
  alt="A puzzle page with the spreadsheet, info panel and chat"
  caption="A puzzle page: the sheet on the left, the info panel and chat on the right."
/>

<Steps>

1. **Open the puzzle itself** with the **Open puzzle** button at the top of the info panel on the right.
2. **Use the sheet.** The left side is a spreadsheet everyone edits at once; you'll see teammates' cursors in their colours. Choose **Puzzle tools** at its top right for formulas like anagram and pattern search.
3. **Draw if you need to.** Switch to the **Whiteboard** above the sheet for grids, maps and scribbles.
4. **Talk in the chat** below the info panel. Type `@` and a name to get a teammate's attention.
5. **Join the call.** In the strip above the chat, choose **Listen in**, then **Unmute and talk here** when you want to speak. You arrive muted.

</Steps>

Your timer chip on the puzzle counts the time you spend with the puzzle's tab in front of you. It feeds your stats and the team's awards.

<Callout type="phone">
  On a phone, the puzzle page shows **Sheet**, **Whiteboard**, **Chat** and **Info** as tabs instead
  of side by side. See [WOW on your phone](/docs/guides/phone).
</Callout>

## Call in an answer

Think you have it? How your team calls in answers is up to them, but WOW has two ways to record one:

- **Queue a guess**: open the **Guesses** section in the info panel, type the answer and choose **Queue**. Whoever is calling in answers marks it **Submitted**, then **Correct** or **Wrong**. WOW warns you if the answer was already tried. See [Calling in answers](/docs/guides/guess-queue).
- **Fill in the answer**: if the hunt has confirmed it, type it in the **Answer** box. That marks the puzzle **Solved**.

Either way, everyone gets a solve pop-up with confetti.

## Say you helped

When a puzzle you worked on is solved, choose **I helped** in its **Solvers** section (or in the solve pop-up). It's self-reported, and it's how WOW counts puzzles for your stats and the team's awards, so mark it whenever you contributed.
