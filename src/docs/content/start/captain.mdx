You're setting WOW up for your team. This page covers what to do before the hunt: find the hunt, create a workspace, invite everyone, and get the board ready. Whoever creates the workspace becomes its first owner.

## Find the hunt

You don't have to start from a blank form. WOW knows about upcoming puzzle hunts from the Puzzle Hunt Calendar:

- On **My workspaces**, **Hunts coming up** shows a featured hunt and a few more, picked from the series you've played before and what's starting soon.
- **Hunts** in the header opens the full list and calendar at `/hunts`, with search and **Online** / **In person** filters.

Choose **Set up workspace** on a hunt, and the create form opens with the hunt's name, dates, location and website already filled in. See [Find a hunt](/docs/guides/find-a-hunt).

## Create the workspace

<Screenshot
  src={createWorkspaceImage}
  alt="The create workspace form, filled in from a hunt suggestion"
  caption="Picking a hunt from the suggestions fills in its dates, location and website."
/>

<Steps>

1. Choose **Set up workspace** on a hunt, or **Create workspace** on My workspaces.
2. Fill in the form. Required fields are marked with \*.
   - **Team name** and **Event name**. Typing an event name suggests known hunts; picking one fills in the dates, location and website.
   - **Starts** and **Ends**: these drive the hunt clock in the header and the solves chart in the activity log.
   - **Location**, and the **Hunt website**, which becomes a link in the workspace header.
   - **Workspace ID**: the workspace's address, `www.wafflehaus.io/` followed by the ID. It must not already be in use.
   - **Workspace password**: teammates enter it to join.
3. Choose **Create**.

</Steps>

Owners can change the names, dates and location later in <UiPath path="Settings > General > Details" />, and the password in <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />. The hunt website lives with the other header links in <UiPath path="Settings > General > Links" />. The workspace ID can't be changed. More detail: [Create a workspace](/docs/guides/create-a-workspace).

## Connect Google Drive or Discord (optional) <OwnerOnly />

Right after creating, **Set up your workspace** shows two choices, **Puzzle spreadsheets** and **Voice calls**, both set to WOW's own. Leave them and choose **Go to blackboard** if you're not sure; you can change them later in <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />.

- **Google Sheets**: every new puzzle gets a Google Sheet in your team's folder instead of the built-in spreadsheet. Worth it if your team prefers Google Sheets. See [Connect Google Drive](/docs/google-drive).
- **Discord**: WOW keeps a Discord server in step with the board, with a voice channel per puzzle. Worth it if your team already lives in Discord. See [Connect Discord](/docs/discord).

Without either, WOW's built-in spreadsheet and voice calls cover the same ground.

## Invite your team

The quickest way: in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />, paste your teammates' email addresses under **Invite people** and click **Send**. Each gets an email with a link that joins them without the password.

Or share the invitation link, `https://join.wafflehaus.io/` followed by your workspace ID, together with the password. You'll find the link with a copy button in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />, under **Invite people**.

Anyone who opens the link signs in (or creates an account), enters the password and chooses **Join**. They become members. If you'd rather make another captain an owner too, change their role in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />. See [Invite your team](/docs/guides/invite-your-team) and [Members and roles](/docs/guides/members-and-roles).

<Callout type="note">
  Members can see the workspace password in Settings, so any teammate can pass it on.
</Callout>

## Build the board

The blackboard starts empty, with **No rounds yet**. Most hunts release puzzles in rounds as you go, so you'll keep adding to the board during the hunt. Anyone on the team can, not just owners.

<Steps>

1. **Add a round**: choose **Add a round** on the empty board, or **Add round** from the **…** menu in the board's header row.
2. **Add a meta**: in the round's menu, choose **Add a meta**. Give it a name and the link to the puzzle on the hunt site.
3. **Add puzzles**: click **Add puzzle** in the board's toolbar, or the **+** on the round's row, and pick the meta it feeds in **Goes in**. Or choose **Add a feeder** in the meta's menu.
4. **Don't know the meta yet?** Pick **No meta (unassigned)** in **Goes in**, or choose **Add a puzzle (no meta)** in the round's menu. When the meta appears, add it with **Assign unassigned puzzles**, or use **Assign all unassigned puzzles** on the unassigned group.

</Steps>

Always fill in the **Link** so solvers can open the puzzle in one click. See [Rounds, metas and feeders](/docs/concepts/hunt-structure) for how these fit together.

## Make it yours

In <UiPath path="Settings > General" />, set an **Accent color** and a **Workspace emoji** <OwnerOnly />, and add **Links** (the hunt site, your team's answer-submission page, a shared doc) that appear in the header for everyone. See [Make it yours](/docs/guides/customize).

## Hunt-day checklist

Before the hunt starts:

- [ ] **Starts** and **Ends** are set, so the hunt clock counts down and the leaderboard knows when the hunt ends.
- [ ] Everyone has joined. Check the list in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />.
- [ ] Your links are in the header: the hunt site and anywhere you call in answers.
- [ ] Your team agrees on statuses: what **Stuck**, **Needs Eyes** and **Extraction** mean to you. See [Statuses and importance](/docs/reference/statuses).
- [ ] Someone owns calling in answers, using the [guess queue](/docs/guides/guess-queue).
- [ ] Teammates know to mark **I helped** on puzzles they worked on, so the awards are fair.
- [ ] If you're solving on MIT guest Wi-Fi with Discord, have a backup network for voice. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting).

When the hunt opens, add the first round and its puzzles, and post an announcement with **Make an announcement** on the blackboard sidebar to get everyone going.
