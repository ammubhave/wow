If your team already lives on a Discord server, WOW can keep it organised for you. Once connected, WOW creates a voice channel for every open puzzle, grouped by round, and tidies them away as puzzles are solved. Only owners can connect Discord. <OwnerOnly />

## What it does

WOW keeps the server in step with the blackboard. Whenever puzzles or rounds change, it updates the channels it manages:

| On the board                                                      | On Discord                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A round with at least one open puzzle                             | A category named after the round, starting with 🧩                     |
| An open puzzle                                                    | A voice channel in its round's category, named "🧩 Puzzle name"        |
| An open meta                                                      | A voice channel named "🧩 [META] Meta name"                            |
| A puzzle marked Solved or Backsolved, marked Obsolete, or deleted | Its channel is renamed "✅ Puzzle name", then deleted 10 minutes later |
| A round with no open puzzles left                                 | Its category is removed                                                |
| A puzzle moved to another round                                   | Its channel moves to that round's category                             |

The 10-minute delay gives anyone still talking in a solved puzzle's channel time to wrap up, or to move to the next puzzle.

<Callout type="note">
  WOW only touches channels whose names start with 🧩, which are the ones it made. Your server's
  other channels and categories are left alone. Don't rename WOW's channels by hand: a channel
  without the 🧩 is no longer WOW's, and WOW will make a fresh one for the puzzle.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  Channels are matched by name. If you rename a puzzle on the board, WOW makes a new channel with
  the new name, and the old one gets the ✅ treatment and is deleted 10 minutes later. Anyone in the
  old channel will need to hop across.
</Callout>

### Announcements on Discord

When anyone makes an announcement with the blackboard's **Make an announcement** button, the **Make an announcement** dialog has a **Also post it to Discord** choice, listing your server's text channels. Pick one, and the announcement is also posted there as "**Announcement from** _your name_" followed by the message. Leave it on **Don't post it to Discord** to send the announcement only inside WOW. The choice only appears while **Voice calls** is set to **Discord**. See [Chat and announcements](/docs/guides/chat).

## Connect Discord <OwnerOnly />

You need permission to add bots to the Discord server, which in Discord means the **Manage Server** permission.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />, or use the **Set up your workspace** page right after creating the workspace.
2. Under **Voice calls**, choose **Discord** instead of **WOW calls**, then click **Connect with Discord**.
3. Discord asks you to pick a server and approve the bot. Choose your team's server and click **Authorize**.
4. You come back to WOW. The step says "You're successfully connected to Discord" and shows the server's name, which links to the server.

</Steps>

WOW creates channels for the board's open puzzles straight away, then keeps them in step as the board changes.

The bot asks Discord for only what it needs to manage its channels: to see channels and to manage channels.

## Reconnect or disconnect

Once connected:

- **Reconnect** runs the connection again. Use it to move WOW to a different server, or to fix a broken connection.
- **Disconnect** removes Discord from the workspace. WOW asks you to confirm, because it **deletes every channel it made** on the server, the 🧩 voice channels and categories. This can't be undone, though if you connect again later, WOW makes fresh channels for the open puzzles.

Choosing **WOW calls** under **Voice calls** doesn't disconnect anything. WOW just stops updating the Discord channels, which stay as they are, and picks up again if you choose **Discord** later.

Members who aren't owners can see which option is chosen and which server is connected, but can't change them.

<Callout type="warning">
  Disconnecting deletes WOW's channels straight away, even if people are talking in them. Do it
  after the hunt, or warn your team first.
</Callout>

## Discord or WOW's built-in voice?

WOW has its own [voice and screen sharing](/docs/guides/voice), with a lobby and a call on every puzzle, so you don't need Discord to talk. Some teams use both.

|                                        | WOW's built-in voice                                                | Discord                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup                                  | None                                                                | An owner connects a server                           |
| Where you talk                         | In the browser, beside the sheet and chat                           | In the Discord app, or Discord in another tab        |
| Seeing who's on a puzzle               | Voice badges on the board, and the call strip on each puzzle        | Discord's channel list                               |
| Moving between puzzles                 | Your call follows you round the workspace, with a floating call bar | Switch channels in Discord                           |
| Screen sharing                         | Yes, as tiles that can go full screen                               | Yes                                                  |
| Text chat                              | WOW's team and puzzle chats                                         | Discord's text channels, plus announcements from WOW |
| Remote teammates who don't use Discord | Nothing to install                                                  | They need a Discord account and to join your server  |

<Callout type="tip">
  If your team is already on Discord year round, connect it and keep doing what you're used to. If
  you're putting together a team for one hunt, WOW's built-in voice saves everyone an extra sign-up.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### The card says there's a problem with the connection

If the bot was removed from the server, or the server was deleted, the card shows "There is a problem with your Discord connection" with Discord's error. Click **Reconnect** and add the bot to the server again.

### Channels aren't appearing

- Check the card shows the right server.
- Check the bot's role on the server can view and manage channels. A server setting or a role change may have taken that away.
- WOW creates channels one at a time, because Discord limits how fast bots can make them. When you add a whole round at once, give it a minute.

### An announcement didn't reach Discord

The bot needs to be able to send messages in the channel you picked. Check the channel's permissions for the bot's role.

### Discord voice doesn't work on MIT's Wi-Fi

MIT's guest Wi-Fi blocks Discord voice. Use another network, such as a phone hotspot, or use WOW's [built-in voice](/docs/guides/voice) instead. More on the [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting) page.
