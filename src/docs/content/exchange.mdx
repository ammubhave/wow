The Wafflehaüs Puzzle Exchange is a free-standing set of puzzles on the WOW site, separate from any workspace. Every month, Team Wafflehaüs releases a few short, approachable, hunt-style puzzles written by team members, and spotlights puzzles from other hunts. Solve them alone or with friends, whenever you like.

You'll find it at [wafflehaus.io/exchange](https://www.wafflehaus.io/exchange).

## Browsing puzzles

The Exchange page has three parts:

| Part                 | What's there                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The newest month** | Featured at the top, with its puzzles one click away                                                                              |
| **Search**           | **Search puzzles by title or month…**: type a puzzle's title or a month to find it. Press <Kbd>/</Kbd> to jump to the search box. |
| **Archive**          | Older months, grouped by year. Only the newest year starts open; click a year to expand it.                                       |

Click a month to see its puzzles, then a puzzle to open it. Search terms and the years you've opened are kept in the page's address, so you can share the link or go back without losing your place.

## Solving a puzzle

Each puzzle page has the puzzle itself, plus a few buttons by its title:

- **Hints**: a menu of hints, each with a short title so you can pick the one you need without spoiling the rest. Choosing one opens it.
- **Solution**: the full write-up. Save it for when you're done, or truly stuck.

### Checking your answer

Type your answer in the box under the puzzle and click **Submit Answer**. Capitals, spaces and punctuation don't matter.

| Result           | What it means                                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Correct!**     | You've solved it. Enjoy the confetti.                                                                         |
| A custom message | You're on the right track: the author anticipated this answer and left a nudge, such as a hint to keep going. |
| **Incorrect**    | Not the answer. Keep at it, or try a hint.                                                                    |

You don't need to be logged in to submit, and there's no limit on guesses.

## Solving with others

Under the puzzles, **Interested in solving?** has two ways to join in:

- **Solve as a team in WOW.** There's a shared WOW workspace for Exchange solvers, so you can use the board, sheets and chat together. The Exchange page shows how to join (click **Open the WPE workspace**). Please be courteous: everyone solving the Exchange shares it.
- **Tell the community.** Once you've solved a puzzle, react to that puzzle's message in the Wafflehaüs Discord.

New to WOW workspaces? See [Quick start for solvers](/docs/start/solver).

## Writing for the Exchange

The Exchange always wants more puzzles. **Interested in writing?** on the Exchange page links to the writing guide at [wafflehaus.io/exchange/writing](https://www.wafflehaus.io/exchange/writing), which covers the writing process (getting started, construction, test solving and polishing) and advice on puzzle writing. To pitch a puzzle, reach out to the Exchange's editors on Discord, as the Exchange page describes.
