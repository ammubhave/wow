Connecting Google Drive is optional. Without it, every puzzle gets WOW's [built-in spreadsheet](/docs/guides/spreadsheet). With it, every new puzzle gets its own Google Sheet in a folder your team owns, opened right inside the puzzle page. Only owners can connect Drive. <OwnerOnly />

## Should you connect it?

Both work well for hunting. The choice is mostly about what your team is used to and what happens to the sheets afterwards.

|                     | Built-in spreadsheet                                                      | Google Sheets                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup               | None                                                                      | An owner connects Google and picks a folder                                                          |
| Who can open it     | Every workspace member, automatically                                     | Members, once WOW has shared the Drive folder with them                                              |
| Accounts            | Just your WOW account                                                     | Each solver needs a Google account with the same email as their WOW account, or must switch accounts |
| Puzzle formulas     | `CAESAR`, `ANAGRAMS`, `ISWORD` and more, plus most Google Sheets formulas | Google Sheets' own formulas, and any add-ons your team uses                                          |
| Puzzle tools        | **Puzzle tools** panel, crossword grids, **Highlight in my color**        | Google's own features                                                                                |
| Following teammates | Click a teammate's avatar to follow their cursor                          | Google's own presence                                                                                |
| Drawings            | The whiteboard on every puzzle                                            | The whiteboard, and also optional Google Drawings                                                    |
| After the hunt      | Stays in WOW                                                              | The files stay in your Google Drive, to keep or share                                                |
| Templates           | Not available                                                             | A template sheet copied for every puzzle                                                             |

<Callout type="tip">
  If your team has a favourite Google Sheets template, with tabs set up the way you like or custom
  scripts, connect Drive. If you'd rather nobody had to think about Google accounts during the hunt,
  skip it. You can always connect it later.
</Callout>

## Connect Google Drive <OwnerOnly />

You can do this on the **Set up your workspace** page right after creating the workspace, or any time in <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />. Under **Puzzle spreadsheets**, choose **Google Sheets** instead of **Built-in sheets**, and three steps appear below.

<Steps>

1. **Connect with Google.** Click **Connect**, choose the Google account that should own the team's files, and allow access. You come back to WOW, and the step says "You're successfully connected to Google."
2. **Select Google Drive Folder.** Click **Select Folder** and pick the folder that every puzzle's file should go in. Google's file picker opens over the page. Once you pick one, the step shows the folder's name as a link.
3. **Select Template File** (optional). Click **Select File** and pick a Google Sheet to copy for each new puzzle. The step then shows the file's name.

</Steps>

Each step unlocks once the one before is done. To change a choice later, use **Reconnect**, **Reselect Folder** or **Reselect File**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Use a new, empty folder that only WOW puts files in. WOW can only manage the files it created or
  that you picked for it. If someone adds another file to the folder by hand, Google won't let WOW
  share the folder with new members, and they won't be able to open the sheets. See
  [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).
</Callout>

### About the template

The template is optional. Without one, each puzzle gets a blank Google Sheet. With one, WOW copies the template, so every puzzle starts with your team's tabs, headings and formulas already in place.

- The template must be a Google Sheet. It's only used for spreadsheets, not drawings.
- It can live anywhere in your Drive. It doesn't need to be in the puzzle folder.
- Changes to the template only affect puzzles added afterwards.

## What happens for each puzzle

Once Drive is connected, the **Add a puzzle** dialog gains a **Worksheet type** choice (a meta added with **Add a meta** always gets a Google Spreadsheet):

| Worksheet type         | What WOW creates                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Google Spreadsheet** | A copy of your template, or a blank Google Sheet if you have no template.                                            |
| **Google Drawing**     | A blank Google Drawing, for puzzles that are more about pictures than grids. The puzzle page's tab says **Drawing**. |

The file is named after the puzzle and saved in your folder. The puzzle page then shows the Google file where the built-in sheet would be. If Google is slow to load, use **Retry**, or **Open in a new tab** to work on it directly in Google. The **Open spreadsheet in a new tab** icon in the puzzle's info panel does the same any time.

<Callout type="note">
  Puzzles only get a Google file if Drive is connected when they're added. Puzzles added earlier
  keep the built-in spreadsheet. If you plan to use Drive, connect it before the hunt starts.
</Callout>

## Member access

WOW shares the Drive folder with each member, so the sheets inside open for them.

- **When someone joins**, WOW gives the email address of their WOW account edit access to the folder.
- **If that fails**, every owner gets a notification that always pops up, with **Open folder** (or **Open the file**, if one file is causing the problem) so you can fix it.
- **Share with everyone**, below the steps in <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />, shares the folder with every member again. It reports how many members it shared with, and lists anyone it couldn't share with and why. Use it:
  - after fixing whatever stopped sharing,
  - for people who joined before you chose the folder,
  - or whenever a teammate says they can't open the sheets.

Sharing again with someone who already has access does nothing, so it's always safe to click.

<Callout type="note">
  Members who aren't owners see the **Puzzle spreadsheets** card in{" "}
  <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />, with its steps and the folder and template names, but
  the buttons are disabled for them and **Share with everyone** is hidden.
</Callout>

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

To change the connection:

- **Reconnect** with a different Google account, or with the same one if WOW's access expired. New puzzles' files then go through that account.
- **Reselect Folder** to send new puzzles' files to a different folder.

- **Built-in sheets**, under **Puzzle spreadsheets**, gives new puzzles the built-in spreadsheet. It doesn't disconnect Drive, so puzzles that already have a Google Sheet keep working, and choosing **Google Sheets** again picks up where you left off.

Puzzles that already have a Google file keep it whatever you change. Their files stay wherever they were created.

If you remove WOW's access from your Google account's security settings, WOW can't create Google files any more until an owner reconnects. Existing puzzles keep their Google files.

## Troubleshooting

### A teammate sees a 403 or "You need access"

This usually means they're signed in to Google in their browser with a different account from the email they joined WOW with. Google is checking the wrong account.

<Steps>

1. In the puzzle's info panel, click **Open spreadsheet in a new tab**.
2. In the Google tab, click the avatar at the top right and switch to the account that matches their WOW email.
3. Come back to WOW and reload the puzzle.

</Steps>

If they're on the right account and it still fails, ask an owner to click **Share with everyone**.

### Sharing fails for everyone

If **Share with everyone** says a file in the folder was added outside WOW, move that file out of the folder in Google Drive (the message links to it when it can), then click **Share with everyone** again.

### "WOW's connection to Google Drive has expired"

The Google connection needs renewing. An owner should click **Reconnect** on the first step, sign in to Google again, then click **Share with everyone**.

### New puzzles aren't getting Google Sheets

Check the **Puzzle spreadsheets** card in <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />. If the first step isn't marked as connected, the connection has lapsed: click **Reconnect**.

More fixes are on the [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting) page.
