WOW (Wafflehaüs Organized Workspaces) is a shared workspace for a puzzle hunt team. Everyone on the team sees the same board of rounds and puzzles, works on the same spreadsheets, and hears about solves the moment they happen.

## What WOW is

A team makes one **workspace** for each hunt. Inside it you get:

- **The blackboard**: every round, meta and puzzle in one table, with answers, statuses, tags and who's working on what.
- **A page per puzzle**: a live spreadsheet, a whiteboard and a chat, side by side, plus the puzzle's answer, notes and solvers.
- **Voice**: a lobby call for the whole team and a call on every puzzle. You keep your call as you move around.
- **Notifications**: solves, announcements and @mentions pop up for everyone, and land in the bell.
- **A guess queue**: line up answers to call in, record what the hunt said, and never repeat a wrong guess.
- **A leaderboard**: live standings and awards during the hunt, and a recap card you can share afterwards.
- **A hunts calendar**: upcoming puzzle hunts in a list or calendar, so you can set up a workspace before the hunt starts.

Everything updates live. When a teammate types an answer, changes a status or edits a sheet, you see it without refreshing.

## The 60-second tour

### My workspaces

After you sign in you land on **My workspaces**. It lists every workspace you're in, with the hunt's dates and how many puzzles are solved. Below it, **Hunts coming up** suggests hunts to set up a workspace for, and the **Join a workspace** card takes a workspace ID and password.

### The blackboard

Open a workspace and you're on the blackboard. Rounds are the top-level rows; under each round are its metas, and under each meta the puzzles that feed it. Click an answer cell to type an answer, or change a status or tags right in the row. The **Working on this** column shows who has each puzzle open. Press <Kbd>/</Kbd> to search.

On the right, the sidebar holds the team's shared notes, a progress grid, and the team chat.

### A puzzle page

Click a puzzle's name to open its page. The spreadsheet (or whiteboard) fills the left; on the right, the info panel shows the answer, status, solvers and guesses, with the puzzle's chat below. Join the puzzle's call from the strip above the chat.

### The header

Across the top: icon tabs for the **Blackboard**, **Settings**, **Activity log**, **Leaderboard** and **Help**, plus a tab for the puzzle you're on. On the right are your team's links, the hunt clock, the notification bell, the lobby headphones button and your account menu. Press <Kbd>⌘ K</Kbd> (or <Kbd>Ctrl K</Kbd>) anywhere in a workspace to jump to any puzzle, page or link.

## How these docs are organised

The sidebar follows a hunt from start to finish:

- **Start here**: this page, the two quick starts, and the tutorial.
- **Concepts**: the ideas behind WOW: workspaces and roles, how rounds, metas and feeders fit together, and how live collaboration works.
- **Before the hunt**: finding a hunt, creating a workspace, inviting your team, Google Drive and Discord, and making the workspace your own.
- **During the hunt**: the blackboard, puzzle pages, the spreadsheet, whiteboard, chat, voice, the guess queue, metas, notifications and phones.
- **After the hunt**: the leaderboard and recap, the activity log, and your stats.
- **Reference**: formulas, keyboard shortcuts, statuses, permissions, notification types, Eggö's commands, your account and a glossary.
- **Help**: troubleshooting and the FAQ.

The same docs are in every workspace, under the **Help** tab in the header, so you don't have to leave your hunt to look something up. Press <Kbd>/</Kbd> on any docs page to search them.
