Quick answers to the questions people ask most. For things that aren't working, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting).

## Getting started

### Is WOW free?

Yes. WOW is free to use, and run by a small group of volunteers. If you'd like to chip in, any amount helps keep it that way: <ChipIn />

### Do I need Google or Discord?

No. Both are optional, and only an owner sets them up. Without Google Drive, every puzzle gets WOW's [built-in spreadsheet](/docs/guides/spreadsheet), which the whole team edits live. Without Discord, you can still talk in WOW's own [voice calls](/docs/guides/voice). You can sign up with just an email and password too.

### Who can see our workspace?

Only its members. To join, someone needs the workspace ID and password, or the invitation link and password. Your board, sheets, chats and calls aren't public.

### Can I be in several workspaces?

Yes. Teams usually make one workspace per hunt, and you can be in as many as you like, on as many teams as you like. Switch between them from **My workspaces**, or click the workspace's name in the footer for a menu of your other workspaces.

## During the hunt

### Is there a mobile app?

No, but WOW works in your phone's browser. The board shows fewer columns, and a puzzle page splits into **Sheet**, **Whiteboard**, **Chat** and **Info** tabs. See [WOW on your phone](/docs/guides/phone).

### How do I assign a puzzle to someone?

WOW doesn't assign puzzles. Instead, you can see who's on each puzzle live in the **Working on this** column, and people mark **I helped** on puzzles they worked on. Use chat, an @mention or the puzzle's **Needs eyes** status to ask for help.

### How is time on puzzles counted?

Your timer on a puzzle page counts only while that puzzle is in the tab you're looking at: the tab is visible and the browser window has focus. It pauses if you're idle for 5 minutes (30 minutes while you're working inside an embedded Google sheet, since WOW can't see your typing there). It's saved every minute. Your time feeds [your stats](/docs/guides/your-stats), the time-based awards on the [leaderboard](/docs/guides/leaderboard), and the **I helped** prompt when a puzzle is solved.

### Do I have to mark "I helped" myself?

Yes. It's self-reported: toggle **I helped** in the puzzle's info panel, or click **I helped** on the solve pop-up. It counts toward the MVP and Meta Maestro awards and your stats.

### Can I use the puzzle formulas in Google Sheets?

No. `MATCHES`, `ANAGRAMS` and the other puzzle formulas only work in WOW's built-in sheet. See [Spreadsheet formulas](/docs/reference/formulas).

## Workspaces and accounts

### Can I delete a workspace?

Not yourself, yet; contact support if you need a workspace deleted. An owner can [archive](/docs/concepts/workspaces#archiving) it instead, which makes it read-only and hides it from everyone's list. You can always leave a workspace from <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />.

### How do I make someone else an owner?

An owner can change anyone's role in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />. See [Members and roles](/docs/guides/members-and-roles).

### Can I delete my account?

Yes, from the **Delete account** card on the Account page. See [Your account](/docs/reference/account).

## Feedback

### How do I get help, report a bug or suggest a feature?

- **In the app:** open the account menu (your avatar, top right) and choose **Support & feedback**, or click the chat button in the corner of the page. It opens a chat with the WOW team; our replies show up there, so you can keep solving while you wait. WOW is run by a small volunteer team, so a reply can take 24 hours or more.
- **On GitHub:** file bugs and feature requests at [github.com/ammubhave/wow/issues](https://github.com/ammubhave/wow/issues).
