WOW tells you about six kinds of event. Each one lands in the **bell** in the header, and can also pop up in the corner of the page and, if you turn it on, as a desktop notification. For a how-to, see [Notifications](/docs/guides/notifications).

## Notification types

| Type                         | Who gets it                | Pop-up                             | Bell                       | Desktop | Clicking it                                                  | Setting that controls the pop-up |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| **Solved** / **Meta solved** | Everyone in the workspace  | ✓, with confetti                   | ✓                          | ✓       | Opens the puzzle                                             | "A puzzle is solved"             |
| **Announcement**             | Everyone in the workspace  | ✓, stays until you close it        | ✓                          | ✓       | Nothing: the message is the point                            | "There's an announcement"        |
| **Mention**                  | Only the person @mentioned | ✓, with **Open**                   | ✓, also under **Mentions** | ✓       | Opens the puzzle's chat, or the blackboard for the team chat | "Someone mentions you"           |
| **Email**                    | Everyone in the workspace  | ✓, stays until you close it        | ✓                          | ✓       | Opens the email in the Inbox                                 | "The workspace gets an email"    |
| **Starred puzzle**           | Whoever starred the puzzle | ✓, with **Open**                   | ✓                          | ✓       | Opens the puzzle                                             | "A puzzle you starred changes"   |
| **Drive sharing failed**     | Owners only                | ✓ always, stays until you close it | ✓                          | ✓       | **Open folder** or **Open the file** in Google Drive         | None: it always pops up          |

### What each one says

- **Solved**: "Solved: _puzzle name_" (or "Meta solved: …") with the answer. It's sent when a puzzle moves to Solved or Backsolved, whoever did it.
- **Announcement**: "Announcement from _name_" with the message. Anyone can send one with **Make an announcement** in the blackboard's sidebar.
- **Mention**: "_Name_ mentioned you in _puzzle_" (or "Team chat") with the start of the message. Editing a message to add a mention notifies the new person; you never get notified about mentioning yourself.
- **Email**: "Email from _sender_" with the subject and the start of the email. It's sent for every email to the workspace's address; see [The workspace inbox](/docs/guides/inbox).
- **Starred puzzle**: "_puzzle_: Now _status_", "_puzzle_: Guess queued: _answer_", "_puzzle_: _answer_ was wrong" or "… keep going (partial)", with who did it, and a star icon. Sent to everyone who starred the puzzle except whoever made the change. Solves aren't sent this way, since everyone gets those.
- **Drive sharing failed**: "Couldn't share the Google Drive folder with _name_", with their email so an owner can share it by hand. When a particular file is blocking the share, the button opens that file.

### The "I helped" button

A solve pop-up includes an **I helped** button when you might have worked on it: you're on that puzzle's page, or you've spent at least a minute on it, and you haven't already marked yourself as a helper. Clicking it adds you to the puzzle's solvers, which counts toward [your stats](/docs/guides/your-stats) and the [leaderboard](/docs/guides/leaderboard).

## Where the settings live

| Setting                                                                                                                                      | Where            | Applies to               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| Pop up when a puzzle is solved / there's an announcement / someone mentions you / the workspace gets an email / a puzzle you starred changes | Bell > gear icon | This browser             |
| Desktop notifications when the tab is hidden (off by default)                                                                                | Bell > gear icon | This browser             |
| **Notifications**: Enabled / Disabled                                                                                                        | Account menu     | Your account, everywhere |

- Turning **Notifications** to **Disabled** in the account menu silences every pop-up and desktop notification. The bell still collects everything.
- Desktop notifications follow the pop-up settings: you only get one for a type that's set to pop up, and only while the WOW tab is hidden. Your browser asks for permission the first time you turn them on.
- The bell lists unread notifications; **Show all notifications** brings back the ones you've read.
- The bell's read state is kept for your account, so what you've read on your laptop is read on your phone too.

## While you were away

If you come back to a workspace after 30 minutes or more away, a "While you were away" summary pops up: how many solves, new rounds, announcements, emails and mentions you missed. Click **Show** to open the bell.
