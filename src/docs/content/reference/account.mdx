Your WOW account is yours, not your team's: one account works across every workspace you're in. You manage it from the **Account** page, which you reach from the account menu (your avatar, top right).

## Signing up

On the sign-up page, either:

- click **Sign up with Google**, or
- enter your **Name**, **Email** and **Password**, then click **Create account**.

Your email is used for Google Drive sharing and password resets, so use the address your team will share Drive files with. If you arrived from an invitation link, you go straight back to it after signing up.

## Logging in

| Method             | How                                                                               |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google             | **Continue with Google**                                                          |
| Passkey            | **Continue with a passkey**, or pick your passkey from the email field's autofill |
| Email and password | Enter them under "or with email"                                                  |

A **Last used** chip marks the method you used last time in this browser, so you don't have to remember.

### Resetting your password

<Steps>

1. On the login page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email, choose a new password under **Set a new password**, and click **Update password**.
4. Log in with your new password.

</Steps>

## The Account page

The Account page (`/profile`) has five cards.

| Card                | What you can do                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Profile**         | Change your **Name** (shown to teammates) and **Email**. A new email only takes effect once you confirm it from the message sent to that address. Your picture comes from [Gravatar](https://gravatar.com/profile); change it there. |
| **Sign-in methods** | **Connect** or **Disconnect** Google. **Change password**, which signs out your other devices. If you only use Google, **Set a password** sends you an email link to add one. You can't remove your last way to log in.              |
| **Passkeys**        | **Add a passkey** to log in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN, or remove one you no longer use.                                                                                                                              |
| **Sessions**        | Every device where you're logged in, with its browser and operating system; "This device" is marked. **Sign out** of any one, or **Sign out of all other devices**. Handy after a hunt on a borrowed computer.                       |
| **Delete account**  | Permanently delete your account and leave all your workspaces.                                                                                                                                                                       |

### Deleting your account

Deleting your account can't be undone. You'll be asked for your password (if you have one) and to type `delete` to confirm. Your past activity in workspaces stays, so your team's history still makes sense, but it shows as "Deleted user".

## The account menu

Click your avatar at the top right, anywhere in WOW.

| Item                | What it does                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Theme**           | Light, Dark, or System (follow your device)                                                                                                                   |
| **Notifications**   | **Enabled** or **Disabled**: a master switch for all pop-ups and desktop notifications, for your account on every device. The bell still collects everything. |
| **My stats**        | [Your stats](/docs/guides/your-stats) across every hunt                                                                                                       |
| **Account**         | The Account page                                                                                                                                              |
| **Report a bug**    | Tell us "What went wrong?" and click **Send**. We'll see which page you were on.                                                                              |
| **Buy me a puzzle** | Support WOW's maker                                                                                                                                           |
| **Log out**         | Log out of this browser                                                                                                                                       |
