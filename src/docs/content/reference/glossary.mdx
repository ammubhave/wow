Puzzle hunts have their own vocabulary, and WOW adds a few words of its own. Terms are in alphabetical order.

## Terms

| Term                   | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Accent colour**      | The colour a workspace uses for highlights, set by an owner in <UiPath path="Settings > General > Look" />.                                                                                   |
| **Announcement**       | A message to the whole team that pops up for everyone and lands in the bell, sent from the megaphone in the blackboard's sidebar. It can also be posted to a Discord channel.                 |
| **Answer**             | The word or phrase a puzzle resolves to, usually in capitals. Some puzzles have several; see [Rounds, metas and feeders](/docs/concepts/hunt-structure).                                      |
| **Backoff**            | A wait some hunts impose after wrong guesses before you can guess again. The guess queue's **Back off…** option counts it down for you.                                                       |
| **Backsolve**          | Working out a puzzle's answer from the other direction, usually from its meta, instead of solving the puzzle itself. WOW has a **Backsolved** status for it, which counts as solved.          |
| **Blackboard**         | The workspace's main page: a table of every round, meta and puzzle, with answers, statuses and who's working on what. See [The blackboard](/docs/blackboard).                                 |
| **Call in**            | Submit an answer to the hunt. Some hunts take answers on their website; others by phone or email.                                                                                             |
| **Caller**             | Whoever on your team submits answers to the hunt. The [guess queue](/docs/guides/guess-queue) lines up guesses for them.                                                                      |
| **Command palette**    | The search box opened with <Kbd>⌘ K</Kbd> / <Kbd>Ctrl K</Kbd> that jumps to any puzzle, page or link.                                                                                         |
| **Extraction**         | The last step of most puzzles: turning what you've worked out (a grid, a list of words) into the answer, often by taking certain letters. Also a WOW status.                                  |
| **Feeder**             | A puzzle whose answer is used in a meta. On the blackboard, feeders sit under their meta.                                                                                                     |
| **Flavor text**        | The short, often italic, text at the top of a puzzle. It usually hints at how to solve it.                                                                                                    |
| **Guess**              | An answer you think might be right but haven't confirmed. WOW tracks guesses in the guess queue so nobody tries the same wrong one twice.                                                     |
| **Guess queue**        | WOW's list of guesses waiting to be called in, and what the hunt said about each: Correct, Wrong or Keep going. See [Calling in answers](/docs/guides/guess-queue).                           |
| **Hunt**               | A puzzle hunt: an event, often over a weekend, where teams solve a large set of puzzles. In WOW, a hunt is also a listing on the hunts calendar. See [Find a hunt](/docs/guides/find-a-hunt). |
| **Hunt clock**         | The countdown in the workspace header, shown once an owner sets the hunt's start and end times.                                                                                               |
| **I helped**           | The toggle on a puzzle page (and button on a solve pop-up) you use to say you worked on a puzzle. It's self-reported and feeds stats and awards.                                              |
| **Importance**         | How much a puzzle matters right now: Important, Normal or Obsolete. See [Statuses and importance](/docs/reference/statuses).                                                                  |
| **Invitation link**    | A link like `https://join.wafflehaus.io/your-id` that takes a teammate to the workspace's join page.                                                                                          |
| **Keep going**         | A hunt's reply that a guess is on the right track but not the final answer (a "partial").                                                                                                     |
| **Lobby**              | The workspace-wide voice call, joined with the headphones button in the header. See [Voice and screen sharing](/docs/guides/voice).                                                           |
| **Member**             | Anyone who has joined a workspace. Members can do almost everything; see [Permissions](/docs/reference/permissions).                                                                          |
| **Meta**               | A meta puzzle: a puzzle whose inputs are the answers to other puzzles (its feeders). Solving metas usually finishes a round.                                                                  |
| **Metameta**           | A meta whose feeders are themselves metas, often the hunt's final puzzle. WOW lets a meta feed another meta.                                                                                  |
| **Owner**              | A member who can also change the workspace's setup: details, look, password, Google Drive, Discord and roles.                                                                                 |
| **Partial**            | See **Keep going**.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Presence**           | Seeing who's where, live: avatars in **Working on this** on the blackboard, and in the strip above each chat.                                                                                 |
| **Round**              | A group of puzzles released together, usually with its own meta or metas. Rounds are the top-level rows on the blackboard.                                                                    |
| **Runaround**          | A hunt's finale, often physical: a sequence of tasks or locations after the final meta.                                                                                                       |
| **Solver**             | Anyone who worked on a puzzle. A puzzle's **Solvers** are the people who've marked **I helped**.                                                                                              |
| **Status**             | Where solving stands on a puzzle, from None to Solved. See [Statuses and importance](/docs/reference/statuses).                                                                               |
| **Tag**                | A label on a puzzle (for example a puzzle type) used to filter the blackboard.                                                                                                                |
| **Team emoji**         | Two things: the emoji an owner picks as the workspace's logo in the header, and the custom reaction emoji anyone can add in Settings for chat.                                                |
| **Unassigned puzzle**  | A puzzle in a round that isn't attached to a meta yet.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Unlock**             | When a hunt releases new puzzles or rounds to your team, usually after you solve enough.                                                                                                      |
| **Workspace**          | Your team's space in WOW for one hunt: its board, sheets, chats, calls and settings. See [Workspaces, owners and members](/docs/concepts/workspaces).                                         |
| **Workspace ID**       | The short name in a workspace's address (`www.wafflehaus.io/<id>`), chosen when it's created. Teammates join with it and the password.                                                        |
| **Workspace password** | The password teammates enter to join. Any member can view it; only owners can change it.                                                                                                      |
