Every puzzle has a **status** (where solving stands) and an **importance** (how much it matters right now). Anyone on the team can change either, from the row on the blackboard or the info panel on the puzzle page. WOW doesn't enforce any meaning beyond the few rules below, so treat the "use it when" column as a starting point and agree on your own conventions as a team.

### Colours on the board

Statuses come in four groups, and the group sets the row's colour on the blackboard and the square's colour in the sidebar's progress grid:

| Group   | Statuses                   | On the board                                                                  |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active  | None, Active               | No fill                                                                       |
| Solved  | Solved, Backsolved         | Text greyed out, with a check next to the name, so unsolved puzzles stand out |
| Warning | Needs eyes, Extraction     | Yellow                                                                        |
| Stuck   | Stuck, Pending, Very stuck | Red                                                                           |

Scan for yellow and red to see where help is needed.

### What changes the status for you

- Typing an answer, in the **Answer** box on a puzzle page or the **Answer** cell on the blackboard, marks it **Solved**. On a puzzle with several answers, it's solved once all of them are in.
- Marking a guess **Correct** in the [guess queue](/docs/guides/guess-queue) writes the answer and marks the puzzle **Solved**.

Moving a puzzle to **Solved** or **Backsolved** sends the solve notification (with confetti) to the whole team, and every status change appears in the [activity log](/docs/guides/activity-log).

Importance only styles unsolved puzzles. Once a puzzle is solved, its row goes quiet whatever its importance.

## Round status

Rounds have a simpler status of their own: **None** or **Solved**. Mark a round solved once you're done with it (typically when its metas are solved). A solved round shows a check by its name. WOW doesn't set it for you, and it's separate from the round's progress bar, which counts solved puzzles.

## Filters that use them

The blackboard's toolbar has toggles built on status and importance. They're saved in your browser, so they only change your view.

| Toggle                | Hides                                                      |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hide solved**       | Puzzles that are Solved or Backsolved                      |
| **Hide solved metas** | Metas marked Solved, along with the puzzles that feed them |
| **Hide obsolete**     | Puzzles with importance Obsolete                           |

The **Importance** dropdown shows only the levels you pick.

## Discord channels

If your team has [connected Discord](/docs/discord), the status also keeps your server tidy. When a puzzle becomes **Solved** or **Backsolved**, or its importance is set to **Obsolete**, its voice channel is renamed "✅ …" and deleted 10 minutes later. Change it back and the channel returns. A round's category goes once none of its puzzles are left.
