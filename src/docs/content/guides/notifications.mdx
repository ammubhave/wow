WOW lets you know when something happens that you'd want to hear about: a puzzle is solved, someone makes an announcement, the workspace gets an email, or a teammate mentions you. You can see everything in the bell, choose what pops up, and get desktop notifications when WOW is in the background.

For a full list of notification types, who gets each one and where it takes you, see [Notification types](/docs/reference/notifications).

## What you'll be notified about

| Notification                           | Who gets it          | What it looks like                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Solved** / **Meta solved**           | Everyone             | "Solved: Puzzle name" with the answer, and confetti.                                               |
| **Announcement**                       | Everyone             | "Announcement from Name" with the message. The pop-up stays until you dismiss it.                  |
| **Mention**                            | The person mentioned | "Name mentioned you in Puzzle", with an **Open** button that takes you to that chat.               |
| **Email**                              | Everyone             | "Email from Sender" with the subject. **Open** takes you to it in the [Inbox](/docs/guides/inbox). |
| **Starred puzzle**                     | Whoever starred it   | "★ Puzzle: Now Stuck", "Guess queued: TEAPOT" or "TEAPOT was wrong", with an **Open** button.      |
| **Drive sharing failed** <OwnerOnly /> | Owners               | WOW couldn't give a new member access to the Google Drive folder.                                  |

## The bell

The bell in the header collects your notifications. A red badge shows how many are unread.

<Screenshot
  src={notificationsImage}
  alt="The notification bell"
  caption="The bell lists solves, announcements and your mentions."
/>

Click it to open the list:

- **All** and **Mentions** tabs at the top. **Mentions** shows only the times someone @-mentioned you.
- The list shows unread notifications. Click **Show all notifications** at the bottom to see the ones you've read too, and **Show unread only** to go back.
- Click a notification to mark it read and go to it: a solve opens the puzzle, a mention opens that puzzle's chat (or the blackboard, for the team chat), and an email opens it in the Inbox.
- **Mark all as read** clears the lot.

Read state is kept with your account, so when you read something on your laptop, it's read on your phone too.

## Puzzles you starred

Star a puzzle on the [blackboard](/docs/blackboard#favourites) and WOW keeps you posted on it, even when you're working on something else. You're told when:

- its status changes (say, to **Stuck** or **Extraction**), other than being solved, which everyone hears about anyway;
- someone queues a guess for it in the [guess queue](/docs/guides/guess-queue);
- a guess comes back wrong, or partial (**Keep going**).

You don't get told about your own changes. Unstar the puzzle to stop. To keep them in the bell without pop-ups, turn off **A puzzle you starred changes** in the settings below.

## Pop-ups

New notifications also pop up in the corner of the screen. Choose which kinds:

<Steps>

1. Open the bell.
2. Click the gear (**Notification settings**).
3. Under **Pop up when…**, turn each kind on or off: **A puzzle is solved**, **There's an announcement**, **Someone mentions you**, **The workspace gets an email**, **A puzzle you starred changes**.

</Steps>

All of them are on by default. Notifications still collect in the bell whatever you choose here; these switches only control pop-ups.

These settings are saved in your browser, so you can have different ones on different devices: quiet on your laptop, everything on your phone.

<Callout type="note">
  Owners always get a pop-up when Drive sharing fails, whatever these settings say, because it needs
  someone to act.
</Callout>

## Desktop notifications

If you keep WOW in a background tab while you work in a Google Sheet or on the hunt site, turn on desktop notifications:

<Steps>

1. Open the bell and click the gear.
2. Turn on **Desktop notifications when the tab is hidden**.
3. Your browser asks for permission to show notifications. Click **Allow**.

</Steps>

You'll then get a system notification, as well as the usual pop-up, for each kind you've chosen to pop up, but only while the WOW tab is hidden. When you're looking at WOW, the pop-up is enough. Click a desktop notification to jump back to WOW and to what it's about.

It's off by default. If you blocked notifications for the site earlier, WOW says "Your browser blocked notifications for this site." Allow them in your browser's site settings, then try again.

## While you were away

If you've been away from a workspace for 30 minutes or more, WOW greets you with a **While you were away** summary when you come back: how many solves, new rounds and announcements there were, and whether you were mentioned. Click **Show** to open the bell and see the details.

## Turn off all pop-ups

To silence every pop-up, in every workspace, on every device:

<Steps>

1. Click your avatar at the top right to open the account menu.
2. Under **Notifications**, choose **Disabled**.

</Steps>

Your notifications still collect in the bell. Choose **Enabled** to turn pop-ups back on.

## The "I helped" prompt

When a puzzle is solved, the solve pop-up includes an **I helped** button if you might have helped: you have the puzzle open, or you've spent at least a minute on it (by your [puzzle timer](/docs/guides/puzzle-page#your-timer)). Click it to add yourself to the puzzle's solvers.

It won't ask if you've already marked yourself. You can always use the **I helped** toggle on the puzzle page instead. Your "I helped" marks count towards the [leaderboard](/docs/guides/leaderboard) awards and [your stats](/docs/guides/your-stats).

## Drive sharing alerts <OwnerOnly />

When a new member joins and the workspace has [Google Drive connected](/docs/google-drive), WOW gives them edit access to the team's folder. If that fails, every owner gets a notification: "Couldn't share the Google Drive folder with Name", telling you which email address to share with.

The pop-up stays until you dismiss it, with **Open folder** (or **Open the file**, when a particular file is blocking the share) to fix it in Google Drive.
