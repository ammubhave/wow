The WOW Chrome extension puts your puzzle beside the hunt's own page. On a puzzle page that's on your board, a sidebar shows the puzzle's details, chat, call and who's there, so you can read the puzzle and work with your team without switching tabs. You can also add the page you're on as a new puzzle in one click.

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Add **WOW for puzzle hunts** to Chrome.
2. Click the WOW icon in Chrome's toolbar, then **Sign in to WOW**. A window opens WOW's sign-in page. Sign in as you do on the website: with your email, a passkey or Google. If you're already signed in to WOW in Chrome, the window closes by itself.
3. In the same popup, choose your **Workspace**. The extension works with one workspace at a time: it shows that workspace's puzzles and adds new ones there. If you're only in one workspace, it's chosen for you.

</Steps>

To switch workspaces, open the popup and pick another one. To sign out, click your picture at the top of the popup, then **Sign out**.

## The sidebar

When you open a page whose link matches a puzzle in your workspace, the sidebar opens on the right of the page. The WOW icon in the toolbar shows a check mark on these pages.

It has the same puzzle details as the right side of the [puzzle page](/docs/guides/puzzle-page):

- The puzzle's name, its round (and meta), and the ⋯ menu to edit, move, copy a link to or delete it.
- **Answer**, **Status**, **Importance** and **Tags**, which you can change right there. Changes reach your team straight away, as they do on the website.
- **Solvers**, **Guesses** and **Notes**.
- The puzzle's [chat](/docs/guides/chat), with the people looking at the puzzle and the [call](/docs/guides/voice) controls at its top. You can join the puzzle's call from the sidebar.

**Open in WOW** opens the puzzle's page in WOW in a new tab, with its spreadsheet and whiteboard. Links in the sidebar, such as the round or another puzzle, also open in WOW in a new tab.

To get the sidebar out of the way, click the collapse button at its top right. It shrinks to a small WOW tab on the edge of the page; click the tab to bring it back. The sidebar stays collapsed or open as you move between puzzles, until you change it.

Links match even when they differ a little: `http` or `https`, `www.` or not, a trailing `/` or `index.html`, and anything after `#` don't matter. If a puzzle's link has no `?…` part, it also matches the page with one.

## Add a puzzle in one click

On a hunt's puzzle page that isn't on your board yet:

<Steps>

1. Click the WOW icon in the toolbar.
2. Check the **Name** (taken from the page's heading, or its title) and the **Round** (the round you last added a puzzle to).
3. Click **Add puzzle**.

</Steps>

The puzzle is added with the page as its link, and the sidebar opens for it.

Once you've added a puzzle from the popup, you can also right-click the page and choose **Add this puzzle to WOW**. That adds it right away, to the same round as last time, without opening the popup.

## Privacy

The extension checks the pages you visit against your workspace's puzzle links on your computer. It doesn't send WOW the addresses of the pages you visit. WOW only hears about a page when you add it as a puzzle. The sidebar is part of the extension, not of the hunt's page, so the hunt's site can't see your WOW data.
