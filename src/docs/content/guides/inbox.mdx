Every workspace has its own email address, its workspace ID followed by **@wafflehaus.io** (for example `nightowls@wafflehaus.io`). Give it to the hunt as your team's contact email: everything the hunt sends to it lands in the workspace's **Inbox**, and everyone in the workspace gets a notification, like an [announcement](/docs/guides/chat#announcements).

## Find the address

Open **Inbox** (the tray icon in the header). The address is under the heading, with a button to copy it. It's always in lower case, and capitals don't matter: `NightOwls@wafflehaus.io` reaches the same workspace.

You can add a tag after a `+` to tell senders apart, as in `nightowls+mit2027@wafflehaus.io`. It still goes to the same inbox.

## When an email arrives

- Everyone in the workspace gets an **Email from _sender_** notification with the subject and the start of the email. The pop-up stays until you dismiss it, and **Open** takes you to the email. See [Notifications](/docs/guides/notifications).
- The email is added to the top of the **Inbox**.
- If the workspace is connected to [Discord](/docs/discord) and the server has a text channel called `#email-updates`, WOW posts the email there too.

## Read the inbox

The **Inbox** lists emails newest first, with who sent each one, its subject and when it arrived. Click one to read it beside the list (on a phone, it opens on its own; the back arrow returns to the list).

| In an open email            | What it does                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Text** / **Formatted**    | Emails sent with formatting can be shown either way. **Formatted** shows the email as designed, but without images from the web. |
| Download (the arrow icon)   | Downloads the email exactly as it arrived, as an `.eml` file your mail app can open. Use it to get the email's attachments.      |
| The arrows at the top right | Step to the newer or older email.                                                                                                |

**Search** at the top of the list filters the emails shown by sender, subject and text. Click **Show older emails** at the bottom to load more.

<Callout type="note">
  To keep the workspace safe, a formatted email can't run scripts, and WOW doesn't load its images
  from the web (which would tell the sender someone opened it). Links open in a new tab.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- Anyone can send to the address, so treat what arrives like any email from outside the team.
- Emails over 10 MB are sent back to the sender.
- An [archived](/docs/guides/customize) workspace doesn't take email: senders get it back.
- Emails to an address that isn't a workspace are sent back.
