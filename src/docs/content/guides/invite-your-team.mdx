There are three ways to bring your team in:

- **Invite by email** <OwnerOnly />: each person gets an email with a link to join. No password needed.
- **Share the invitation link** with the workspace password, for example in your team's chat.
- **Share the workspace ID and password** for teammates to type in.

## Invite by email <OwnerOnly />

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />.
2. Under **Invite people**, type or paste email addresses. Put several in at once, separated by commas or new lines.
3. Click **Send invitation**. Each address gets an email from noreply@wafflehaus.io with a **Join the team** button.

</Steps>

The person opens the link, signs in (or signs up) **with the address you invited**, and clicks **Join the team**. They're in, as a member, with no password to type. If they're signed in with a different address, the page says so and offers to switch accounts.

Invitations expire after 7 days. Until they're used, they're listed under **Waiting to join**, where you can **Resend** one (it also gets a fresh 7 days) or cancel it with the ✕. Inviting someone who's already on the team, or already invited, tells you so instead of sending another email.

<Callout type="tip">
  If a teammate signs in with Google under a different address than the one you know, invite that
  address, or send them the invitation link and password instead.
</Callout>

## Share the invitation link

The invitation link is `https://join.wafflehaus.io/<workspace-id>`. For a workspace with the ID `waffles2027`, it's `https://join.wafflehaus.io/waffles2027`.

<Steps>

1. In the workspace, open <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />. Owners find the link in the **Invite people** card, under "Or share the link and password". Everyone else finds it in the **Invite teammates** card.
2. Click the copy button next to the **Link**, and the one next to the **Password**.
3. Paste them into your team's chat or email.

</Steps>

Or click **Copy link with password**: a link that joins in one click, with the password already in it. Anyone who has that link can join, so share it only with your team.

Any member can copy the link and password, not just owners. So if a teammate asks how to get in, anyone already inside can answer.

<Callout type="tip">
  A link to any page inside the workspace works as an invitation too. If a teammate who hasn't
  joined opens a link to the board or a puzzle, WOW shows them the join page first, then takes them
  in.
</Callout>

## What joiners see

When someone opens the invitation link:

<Steps>

1. **If they're not signed in**, WOW asks them to log in. People without an account can click through to sign up, with Google or with a name, email and password. Either way, WOW brings them back to the join page afterwards.
2. **The join page** shows your team's emoji, name and hunt, in your team's colour, and how many teammates are already in, so they can check it's the right one.
3. They type the **Workspace password** and click **Join**. With the link that has the password in it, there's nothing to type: just the button.
4. WOW adds them as a member and opens the blackboard.

</Steps>

If they get the password wrong, WOW says "That password isn't right." and they can try again.

If they're already a member, the join page sends them straight on to the workspace. So it's fine to open the link twice, or to post it somewhere teammates who've already joined will see it.

<Callout type="note">
  Everyone who joins this way is a **member**. Members can do almost everything during a hunt: add
  and edit puzzles, chat, call, and change tags and links. If you want someone else to manage the
  workspace's settings with you, make them an owner in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />. See
  [Members and roles](/docs/guides/members-and-roles).
</Callout>

## Join with the ID and password

If you don't have the link, you can join from **My workspaces** with the ID and password your team shared.

<Screenshot
  src={workspacesImage}
  alt="My workspaces with the Join a workspace card"
  caption="The Join a workspace card sits beside your workspaces."
/>

<Steps>

1. Go to **My workspaces**.
2. Find the **Join a workspace** card. On a wide screen it's beside your list of workspaces. On a narrower screen it's below the list: click **Join workspace** at the top of the page to jump to it.
3. Enter the **Workspace ID** and the **Workspace password**.
4. Click **Join workspace**.

</Steps>

You'll go straight to the workspace's blackboard, and it appears in your list on My workspaces from then on.

<Callout type="phone">
  On a phone, the **Join workspace** button at the top of My workspaces scrolls down to the Join a
  workspace card and puts your cursor in the ID field.
</Callout>

## Google Drive access for members

If the workspace uses [Google Drive](/docs/google-drive), each puzzle's Google Sheet lives in the team's Drive folder. When someone joins, WOW automatically gives their account's email address edit access to that folder, so the sheets open for them without asking.

Things to know:

- **Access goes to the email address of their WOW account.** If someone signs in to WOW with one address but is logged in to Google in their browser with another, the embedded sheets may show a Google "403" or "You need access" error. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting).
- **If sharing fails**, every owner gets a notification that pops up even if pop-ups are turned down, with a link to open the folder or the file causing the problem. The usual cause is a file in the folder that was added outside WOW, which stops WOW sharing the whole folder.
- **Owners can share again with everyone.** In <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />, the **Puzzle spreadsheets** card has a **Share with everyone** button that re-shares the folder with every member. Use it after fixing the cause, or for people who joined before the folder was chosen. Sharing with someone who already has access changes nothing.

## Change the password <OwnerOnly />

Change the password if it's leaked somewhere public, or between hunts if you reuse a workspace.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />.
2. Under **Workspace Password**, type the new password. It must be at least 8 characters.
3. Click **Save**. You'll see "The workspace password has been updated."

</Steps>

Changing the password doesn't remove anyone. People who already joined stay members. It only affects people joining from now on, so let your team know the new one. The invitation link doesn't change, since it only contains the workspace ID.

Members who aren't owners can see the password but not edit it, and the **Save** button doesn't appear for them.

## People already signed in vs new accounts

| Teammate                         | What happens when they open the link                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Already signed in to WOW         | Goes straight to the join page, enters the password, and is in.                            |
| Has an account but is signed out | Logs in (with Google, a passkey, or email and password), then comes back to the join page. |
| New to WOW                       | Clicks through to sign up, creates an account, then comes back to the join page.           |
| Already a member                 | Goes straight to the workspace.                                                            |

<Callout type="tip">
  Ask teammates to sign up with the same email address they use for Google. That way, if your team
  uses Google Drive, the sheets open for them without any account switching.
</Callout>
