WOW works on your phone, so you can check the board, chat with the team or call in an answer when you're away from your laptop. Some things change to fit a small screen, and a few desktop-only tools are left out. This page covers what's different.

The phone layout kicks in on narrow screens (under about 768 pixels wide), so it also applies to a narrow browser window on a computer.

## Getting around

The header shrinks to the essentials. The section tabs and header links move into a menu:

<Steps>

1. Tap the menu button at the top left.
2. Pick where to go. The menu lists the puzzle you're on (or last opened) first, then **Blackboard**, **Settings**, **Activity log**, **Inbox**, **Stats** and **Help**, then your workspace's links.

</Steps>

On a puzzle, its name shows in the header next to the menu button, so you always know where you are.

The rest of the header stays: the guess queue (while guesses are waiting), the notification bell, the lobby headphones button and your account menu. The hunt clock is hidden on phones.

## The blackboard

The [blackboard](/docs/blackboard) shows the same rounds and puzzles, with a few changes:

<Screenshot src={phoneBoardImage} alt="The blackboard on a phone" narrow />

- **Fewer columns**: only **Name** and **Status**, plus the ⋯ menu. A puzzle's answer appears in small text under its name. To type or change an answer, or to see or change its importance and tags, open the puzzle's **Info** tab. To open a puzzle on the hunt site, choose **Open on the hunt site** in its ⋯ menu.
- **Filters in a row**: the search box gets the full width, and the filters (**Hide solved**, **Favorites** and the rest) sit in a row underneath it. Swipe the row sideways to reach them all.
- **Favourites in the menu**: there's no room for the star in the row, so open a puzzle's **⋯** menu and choose **Add to favorites** (or **Remove from favorites**).
- **No Arrange mode**: to move a puzzle to a different meta or round, open its ⋯ menu, choose **Move to…**, pick where it goes in **Goes in** and tap **Move**.
- **Add puzzle** is a **+** button in the toolbar. Round rows have no **+** of their own, and show the solved/total count without the progress bar.

### Drawers for notes and team chat

The board's sidebar doesn't fit on a phone. Instead, two buttons at the right of the toolbar open drawers from the side:

| Button                                 | What it opens                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Notes and progress** (notebook icon) | The team notes, the per-round progress squares, and the **Make an announcement** button. |
| **Team chat** (speech bubbles icon)    | The team chat, with the lobby call strip.                                                |

Tap outside the drawer, or its close button, to go back to the board.

## The puzzle page

On a phone, the [puzzle page](/docs/guides/puzzle-page) shows one thing at a time. Tabs along the top switch between them:

<Screenshot src={phonePuzzleImage} alt="A puzzle's Info tab on a phone" narrow />

| Tab            | What's in it                                                                  |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sheet**      | The puzzle's spreadsheet or Google Sheet.                                     |
| **Whiteboard** | The shared [whiteboard](/docs/guides/whiteboard).                             |
| **Chat**       | The puzzle's chat, with its voice call strip.                                 |
| **Info**       | Answer, status, importance, tags, solvers, guesses, feeder answers and notes. |

Your timer stays at the right end of the tab bar. The tab you're on is part of the page's address (for example `?view=chat`), so sharing the link opens the same tab for a teammate on a phone. On a computer, links to the Chat or Info tab open the sheet, since chat and info are always visible there.

In the built-in spreadsheet the toolbar is more compact, and **Puzzle tools** shows as an icon only.

## Chat

In any chat, there's no hover to reveal a message's actions. **Tap a message** to show them: **Add reaction**, **Pin message**, and for your own messages **Edit** and **Delete**. See [Chat and announcements](/docs/guides/chat).

## What's desktop-only

| Feature                                                                   | On a phone instead                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrange mode on the blackboard                                            | Use **Move to…** in the row's ⋯ menu.                                     |
| Answer, Importance and Tags columns, and presence avatars                 | Open the puzzle's **Info** tab.                                           |
| The hunt clock in the header                                              | Check the hunt's dates in <UiPath path="Settings > General > Details" />. |
| Section tabs and header links                                             | The header menu.                                                          |
| "Jump to a day" contents in the [activity log](/docs/guides/activity-log) | Scroll the log.                                                           |

## Tips

- **Add WOW to your home screen.** Most phone browsers can do this from their share or options menu ("Add to Home Screen"). WOW then opens from an icon like an app.
- **Use your phone for voice.** Join the puzzle's call on your phone and keep your laptop's screen for the sheet. Only one device can be unmuted at a time, so unmuting on your phone mutes you on your laptop, which avoids echo. See [Voice and screen sharing](/docs/guides/voice).
- **Get notified.** Pop-ups only appear while WOW is open. If you want to hear about solves and mentions while away from your laptop, keep WOW open in your phone's browser. See [Notifications](/docs/guides/notifications).
- **Big puzzles are easier on a bigger screen.** The sheet and whiteboard work on a phone, but for heavy grid work, a laptop or tablet is more comfortable.
