Most hunts have you submit answers on their website, and many limit how often you can guess, or make you wait after a wrong answer. WOW's guess queue keeps track of every guess your team wants to make, what the hunt said about each one, and when you're allowed to try again.

## Why use a guess queue

- **Rate limits and backoffs.** When the hunt makes you wait, the queue counts down and tells you when you're **Ready**, so nobody guesses early and gets locked out longer.
- **No repeated guesses.** WOW warns you if an answer has already been tried on this puzzle, so you don't waste a guess on something a teammate already got wrong.
- **One caller.** One person can submit every guess the team has lined up, from a single list in the header, instead of everyone typing into the hunt site at once.
- **A record.** Every guess, who queued it and what happened to it, stays on the puzzle.

## Queue a guess

<Screenshot
  src={guessesImage}
  alt="The Guesses section of a puzzle"
  caption="A puzzle's guesses: one submitted, one wrong."
/>

<Steps>

1. On the [puzzle page](/docs/guides/puzzle-page), find **Guesses** in the info panel. While a puzzle has no guesses it's collapsed: click **Guesses (0)** to open it.
2. Type your answer in **Queue a guess to call in**.
3. Click **Queue** or press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.

</Steps>

The guess appears in the list as **Queued**, with your name and how long ago you added it. The section heading shows how many guesses the puzzle has, and a "N waiting" chip when some are still queued or submitted.

### Already-guessed warning

As you type, WOW checks the puzzle's earlier guesses. If you've typed one that's already been tried, a warning appears, like "Already guessed: wrong (TEAPOT)." or "Already guessed: queued.".

The check ignores case, spaces and punctuation, so `Ice-cream`, `ICE CREAM` and `icecream` all count as the same guess. You can still queue it if you really mean to.

## Guess statuses

| Status         | Meaning                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Queued**     | Waiting to be called in. If the hunt has asked you to back off, it shows a countdown, then **Ready**. |
| **Submitted**  | Called in; waiting for the hunt's reply.                                                              |
| **Correct**    | The hunt accepted it.                                                                                 |
| **Wrong**      | The hunt rejected it.                                                                                 |
| **Keep going** | A partial answer: the hunt said you're on the right track.                                            |
| **Withdrawn**  | Taken back without calling it in. Shown crossed out.                                                  |

Queued and Submitted guesses are "waiting": they show in the header queue until someone records the outcome.

## Record what happened

Each guess has a button for the usual next step, and a ⋯ menu for everything else.

| Button        | Shows on                                  | What it does                                                    |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Submitted** | Queued guesses (once any backoff is over) | Marks it called in.                                             |
| **Correct**   | Submitted guesses                         | Marks it correct, and fills in the puzzle's answer (see below). |

The ⋯ menu has every option:

| Menu item                | What it does                                       |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Submitted**            | Marks it called in.                                |
| **Correct**              | Marks it correct and fills in the answer.          |
| **Wrong**                | Marks it rejected.                                 |
| **Keep going (partial)** | Marks it as a partial answer.                      |
| **Back off…**            | Puts it back in the queue with a wait (see below). |
| **Back to the queue**    | Returns it to **Queued**, without a wait.          |
| **Withdraw**             | Takes it back.                                     |

Anyone in the workspace can update any guess. If you click the wrong one, just pick the right status from the ⋯ menu.

## Back off after a wrong guess

Many hunts make you wait before guessing again, especially after several wrong answers. To record that:

<Steps>

1. Open the guess's ⋯ menu and choose **Back off…**.
2. Pick how long the hunt asked you to wait: 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or 1 hour.

</Steps>

The guess goes back to **Queued** with an hourglass countdown. While it counts down, the **Submitted** button is hidden, so nobody calls it in too early. When the time's up, it shows **Ready** and the **Submitted** button comes back.

## When a guess is correct

Marking a guess **Correct** does more than change its status:

- The answer is written onto the puzzle.
- The puzzle is marked **Solved**.
- Everyone on the team gets the solve pop-up, with confetti, and the puzzle sparkles on the [blackboard](/docs/blackboard).

So the person calling in answers doesn't have to update the board separately.

### Puzzles with several answers

On a puzzle with more than one answer (see [Solving metas](/docs/guides/metas#puzzles-with-more-than-one-answer)), a correct guess is added to the answers found so far, instead of replacing them. The puzzle is only marked Solved once all of its answers are in.

## The header queue

While any guess in the workspace is waiting, a list icon with a count appears in the header. It disappears when nothing is waiting, so it's a good signal that someone needs to act.

<Screenshot
  src={guessQueueImage}
  alt="The workspace guess queue"
  caption="The header queue lists every guess still waiting, across puzzles."
/>

Click it to see every waiting guess in the workspace, oldest first, so none gets forgotten. It's split into:

- **To call in**: queued guesses, ready to submit (or counting down).
- **Waiting on the hunt**: guesses that have been submitted and need their outcome recorded.

Each guess links to its puzzle and has the same buttons and ⋯ menu as on the puzzle page, so you can work through the whole queue without leaving the list.

## A workflow for a designated caller

Many teams pick one person to call in answers. A setup that works well:

<Steps>

1. Solvers queue guesses on the puzzle page as soon as they have them, instead of submitting on the hunt site themselves. Add a note in the puzzle chat if the guess needs explaining ("backsolved from the meta").
2. The caller keeps the header queue open. Working from **To call in**, oldest first, they submit each guess on the hunt site and click **Submitted**.
3. When the hunt replies, the caller records it: **Correct**, **Wrong**, **Keep going (partial)**, or **Back off…** with the wait the hunt gave.
4. If the hunt asks for a phone call or more information, the caller says so in the puzzle's chat, or [mentions](/docs/guides/chat#mention-someone) the solvers.
5. Hand over the role when the caller takes a break. Anyone can pick up the queue, since it's shared.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  Even without a designated caller, queueing every guess first means the team has one place to check
  what's been tried, and the duplicate warning catches repeats.
</Callout>
