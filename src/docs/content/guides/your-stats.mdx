**My stats** adds up your puzzling across every workspace you're in: how many puzzles you've helped solve, how long you've spent on puzzles, when you like to puzzle, and who you solve with. Only you can see your stats.

## Open your stats

<Steps>

1. Click your avatar at the top right of any page.
2. Choose **My stats**.

</Steps>

The page's address is `/stats`, so you can bookmark it.

If you haven't done anything yet, you'll see "Nothing here yet." Open a puzzle and start solving: your time on puzzles, and the puzzles you mark **I helped**, are what fill this page.

## The tiles

The row at the top gives your totals:

| Tile                     | What it counts                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **puzzles helped solve** | Puzzles you marked **I helped** that are solved or backsolved now.               |
| **metas**                | How many of those are metas.                                                     |
| **on puzzles**           | Your total active time on puzzle pages, as hours and minutes, such as "12h 05m". |
| **hunts**                | Workspaces where you've spent time on puzzles or helped solve one.               |

## The cards

Below the tiles, each card appears once there's something to show in it.

### When you puzzle

A bar for each hour of the day, showing how much of your puzzle time falls in that hour, in your local time. The card names your peak: "Most active around 11 PM, your local time." Hover a bar to see its hour and total.

### Favourite puzzle types

Your time on puzzles, by tag, for your top six tags. A puzzle with several tags counts towards each. Untagged puzzles are left out, since they don't say what kind of puzzle they were. This card only fills in if your teams tag their puzzles: see [tags](/docs/guides/customize#tags).

### Who you solve with

Up to eight teammates who marked **I helped** on the same puzzles as you, with how many puzzles you share. It spans every workspace you're in, so a friend you've hunted with for years rises to the top.

### Where your time went

The five puzzles you spent longest on, across all your hunts, with the workspace each belongs to and your time on it. Each puzzle links back to its page.

### By hunt

Each workspace where you've puzzled, newest first, with the event name, how many puzzles you helped solve there, and your time. Click a workspace to open it.

## How time is tracked

Your time on a puzzle counts only while you're actually on it:

- The puzzle's page is open in the browser tab you're looking at. Background tabs don't count.
- The browser window has focus.
- You aren't idle. If you don't move the mouse, type, scroll or touch the screen for five minutes, the clock stops until you come back. Inside an embedded Google Sheet, where WOW can't see your typing, it waits 30 minutes.

Time on the blackboard, in team chat, or in settings doesn't count. Only puzzle pages do.

The **timer** chip on each puzzle page shows your running total for that puzzle. WOW saves your time about once a minute, and right away when you switch away, go idle, or close the page. It also notes the hour on your device's clock, which is what the **When you puzzle** card and the Night Owl and Early Bird awards use.

<Callout type="tip">
  Working from a printout or on paper? WOW can't see that, so it won't count. Your **I helped**
  marks still count, so mark the puzzles you helped with even if your time on them is low.
</Callout>

## What counts and what doesn't

- **Only workspaces you're still in count.** If you [leave a workspace](/docs/guides/members-and-roles#leave-a-workspace), its time and solves drop out of your stats.
- **Only puzzles that are solved now count** towards your solves. If a puzzle is un-solved, it stops counting until it's solved again.
- **Deleted puzzles** drop out entirely: when a puzzle is deleted, your time on it goes with it.
- **"I helped" is self-reported.** WOW doesn't guess who solved what. See [why "I helped" matters](/docs/guides/leaderboard#why-i-helped-matters).

## Stats for one hunt

For one workspace, and to compare with your teammates, open that workspace's **Stats** tab. Its **Your hunt** card shows your numbers and awards for that hunt alone. See [Leaderboard and recap](/docs/guides/leaderboard).

<Callout type="phone">
  My stats works on a phone too: open the account menu from your avatar at the top right and choose
  **My stats**.
</Callout>
