WOW has voice calls built in: a lobby for the whole team and a call on every puzzle. You can listen in, talk, turn on your camera or share your screen, and your call follows you as you move around the workspace.

## Rooms: the lobby and puzzle calls

| Room            | Where to join                                                                            | Use it for                                                  |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lobby**       | The headphones button in the header, or the strip above the team chat on the blackboard. | Hanging out, coordinating, catching people between puzzles. |
| **Puzzle call** | The strip above the puzzle's chat on its [puzzle page](/docs/guides/puzzle-page).        | Working on that puzzle together.                            |

You're in one room at a time. Joining another room moves you there.

The headphones button in the header shows a green badge with how many people are in the lobby; hover it to see who. It turns green when someone there is talking. Click it to **Listen in the lobby**, and again to **Leave the lobby**.

## Join a call

Above each chat there's a strip showing who's here (looking at the puzzle, or on the board for the team chat) and who's in the call. Each person's chip shows their mic, camera and screen-share state, and a ring lights up while they talk. You can see all of this without joining.

To join, use the buttons at the right of the strip:

| Button                   | What it does                                               |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Listen in**            | Joins the call muted, so you can hear without being heard. |
| **Unmute and talk here** | Joins and unmutes you in one click.                        |

<Callout type="note">
  The first time you unmute, your browser asks for permission to use your microphone. If you said
  no, WOW shows "Couldn't use your microphone. Check the browser's permission for this site." Allow
  the microphone in your browser's site settings and try again.
</Callout>

### You arrive muted

Whenever you join or move to a room, you arrive muted, with your camera and screen share off. Nobody hears you until you choose to unmute. If you start talking while muted, a "You're muted" reminder appears.

### Unmuted in one room at a time

You can only be unmuted in one place. If you unmute somewhere else, in another tab or on another device (say, your phone), WOW mutes you in the first one. This stops your voice from going out twice, and stops echo when you have WOW open in several tabs.

## In the call

Once you're in a room, its strip has your controls:

| Button                                   | What it does                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mute** / **Unmute**                    | Turns your microphone off or on.                         |
| **Turn camera on** / **Turn camera off** | Shares your camera.                                      |
| **Share your screen** / **Stop sharing** | Shares a screen, window or tab. Your browser asks which. |
| **Leave call**                           | Hangs up.                                                |

Cameras and shared screens appear as tiles. A shared screen can go fullscreen, which is the easiest way to read someone's sheet over their shoulder.

<Callout type="tip" title="Share a screen to walk through a puzzle">
  Explaining a deduction is often faster when everyone's looking at the same thing. Share your
  screen, or ask teammates to follow you in the [built-in
  spreadsheet](/docs/guides/spreadsheet#follow-a-teammate).
</Callout>

## Keep the call as you move around

Your call stays connected as you go from the board to other puzzles, settings or the activity log. It only ends when you leave it, or leave the workspace.

When you're not on the call's puzzle page (or whenever you're in the lobby), a floating call bar appears near the bottom of the screen. It shows the room's name, avatars of who's in it (ringed while talking), and the same controls: mute, camera, screen share and leave. When someone has a camera or screen on, **Show video** and **Hide video** open and close their tiles.

## See who's talking on the board

On the [blackboard](/docs/blackboard), a puzzle with people in its call shows a headphones icon and a count next to its name. Hover it to see who's there. It pulses while someone's talking. Use it to find where the conversation is, or to avoid barging into a busy call.

## Built-in voice or Discord

Some teams prefer Discord for voice. If an owner [connects Discord](/docs/discord), WOW creates a voice channel for each puzzle on your server ("🧩 Name", with "[META]" in front for metas), grouped by round, and tidies them up as puzzles are solved.

You can use either, or both. Built-in voice needs nothing extra and shows on the board and in the strips; Discord suits teams who already live there. Just agree as a team which one you're using, so people end up in the same place.

<Callout type="warning" title="Discord voice on MIT guest Wi-Fi">
  MIT's guest Wi-Fi blocks Discord voice. If you're solving on campus, use another network or a
  phone hotspot. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting).
</Callout>
