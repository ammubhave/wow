Everyone in a workspace is either an **owner** or a **member**. Members can do almost everything a hunt needs. Owners can also manage the workspace itself. This page covers the **Members** tab, changing roles, and leaving. For the full list of who can do what, see [Permissions](/docs/reference/permissions).

## Owners and members

|                                                     | Members | Owners |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------ |
| Add, edit and delete puzzles and rounds             | Yes     | Yes    |
| Answers, statuses, importance, tags on puzzles      | Yes     | Yes    |
| Chat, calls, announcements, guesses                 | Yes     | Yes    |
| Header links, workspace tags, custom reaction emoji | Yes     | Yes    |
| See the workspace password and invitation link      | Yes     | Yes    |
| Team and event name, hunt dates and location        | No      | Yes    |
| Accent colour and team emoji                        | No      | Yes    |
| Change the workspace password                       | No      | Yes    |
| Connect Google Drive and Discord                    | No      | Yes    |
| Change people's roles                               | No      | Yes    |
| See members' email addresses                        | No      | Yes    |

Whoever creates a workspace is its first owner. Everyone who joins with the password or invitation link is a member.

<Callout type="tip">
  Make at least two people owners: the captain and a deputy. Then if the captain is asleep or away
  when the hunt changes its start time, someone else can update the dates, or reconnect Google Drive
  if it lapses.
</Callout>

## The Members tab

Open <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />. The card's title shows how many people are in the workspace, for example **Members (14)**.

- Owners are listed first, then everyone else, in alphabetical order.
- Each person shows their avatar and name. Your own row says "(you)".
- **If you're an owner**, each row also shows the person's email address, and a role menu (**Owner** or **Member**) on the right.
- **If you're a member**, you see the names, with an **Owner** chip beside each owner, so you know who to ask when something needs changing.

<Callout type="phone">
  On a narrow screen, the settings tabs (**General**, **Members**, **Administration**) run across
  the top of the page instead of down the side.
</Callout>

## Make someone an owner <OwnerOnly />

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />.
2. Find the person, and open the role menu on the right of their row.
3. Choose **Owner**.

</Steps>

You'll see "_Name_ is now an owner." The change takes effect immediately. If they have Settings open already, they may need to reload the page to see the owner-only settings unlock.

To make an owner a member again, choose **Member** in the same menu. You'll see "_Name_ is now a member."

You can change your own role this way too, as long as another owner remains.

### The last owner

A workspace always needs at least one owner, so someone can manage it. If you try to make the only owner a member, WOW refuses with "A workspace needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."

To hand a workspace over to someone else, make them an owner first, then change yourself to **Member**.

## Leave a workspace

Anyone can leave a workspace they no longer need, for example an old hunt cluttering My workspaces.

<Steps>

1. Open <UiPath path="Settings > General" />.
2. Scroll to the bottom, to the **Leave Workspace** card.
3. Click **Leave**.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  There's no confirmation step: clicking **Leave** removes you right away and takes you back to My
  workspaces.
</Callout>

What happens when you leave:

- You can no longer open the workspace, and it disappears from My workspaces.
- Your past activity stays: your chat messages, board changes and "I helped" marks are part of the team's history and still appear for everyone else.
- The workspace no longer counts towards [your stats](/docs/guides/your-stats), which only cover workspaces you're still in.
- If you're the only owner, WOW won't let you leave. Make someone else an owner first.

To come back, join again with the workspace ID and password, or the invitation link. You'll rejoin as a member. See [Invite your team](/docs/guides/invite-your-team).

## What non-owners see

Members can open every settings tab, but some things are read-only for them:

- **General**: a note at the top says "Only owners can change the details and look". The **Details** fields and the **Look** card are greyed out. The custom **Team emoji**, **Links** and **Tags** cards below still work for everyone, and so does the invitation link's copy button.
- **Members**: names and owner chips, without emails, role menus or remove buttons.
- **Administration**: a note says "Only owners can change these settings". Members can still see the **Workspace Password**, so they can tell teammates how to join, and which Google Drive folder and Discord server are connected. The buttons are disabled.

If something needs changing, ask one of the people with the **Owner** chip.

## Removing people and deleting workspaces

Owners can remove someone in <UiPath path="Settings > Members" />: click the remove button next to their role and confirm. They lose access right away, to the workspace and (if connected) its Google Drive folder; the puzzles, answers and messages they added stay. You can't remove yourself this way (use **Leave**).

Someone who's been removed can join again with the workspace password. If they shouldn't, an owner can change it in <UiPath path="Settings > Administration" />, so nobody new can join with the old one.

Owners can [archive](/docs/concepts/workspaces#archiving) a workspace in Administration. Deleting isn't available yet: the **Delete Workspace** card says to contact support.
