WOW is built for a team working on the same hunt at the same moment. Nothing needs refreshing: when a teammate changes something, it changes on your screen too.

## Everything updates live

These all reach everyone in the workspace as they happen:

- **The blackboard**: new rounds and puzzles, answers, statuses, importance, tags and the order of rows.
- **Puzzle pages**: the answer, status, solvers, notes and guesses.
- **Spreadsheets and whiteboards**: every edit, cell by cell and stroke by stroke.
- **Chats**: messages, edits, reactions, pins, and "… is typing".
- **Notifications**: solves, announcements and mentions pop up for everyone they're meant for.

The only things that don't sync are the ones meant to be personal: your filters on the board, filters inside a sheet, and your notification choices.

## Who's where

WOW shows where your teammates are, so you can find a puzzle that needs people, or the person you need.

- **Working on this**: on the blackboard, the avatars in this column show who has each puzzle open right now.
- **Who's online**: the footer shows who's in the workspace, along with the latest activity.
- **Voice**: a voice badge on a board row shows who's in that puzzle's call, and the strip above each chat shows who's viewing the puzzle and who's in its call.
- **In a sheet**: the avatars at the top right are the people in that sheet, and each teammate's selected cells are outlined in their colour with their name.
- **On a whiteboard**: teammates' cursors move live on the canvas.

### Follow a teammate in a sheet

In the built-in spreadsheet, click a teammate's avatar at the top right to jump to where they are and follow along as they move. Click their avatar again, or **Following …** **✕**, to stop. It's handy when someone says "look at this" in a call.

To show the team which part of a sheet you're working on, select the cells, right-click and choose **Highlight in my color**.

## Seeing what changed

When a teammate changes a puzzle's answer, status or importance on the blackboard, the cell glows briefly and their avatar pops up beside the puzzle's name. A solve sparkles. You can follow a busy board out of the corner of your eye.

For anything you missed:

- After 30 minutes or more away, a **While you were away** summary pops up with a **Show** button.
- The [activity log](/docs/guides/activity-log) has every change, day by day.

## Going offline

If your connection drops for more than a second or so, a notice appears at the bottom of the screen: "Reconnecting… teammates' changes will appear once you're back online." When it's back, you'll see **Back online**, and the board catches up.

While you're offline:

- **The built-in spreadsheet** keeps your edits and shows **Offline: edits will sync when you reconnect**. They're sent when you're back. If you were offline for so long that they can't be merged with the team's changes, WOW tells you they were dropped, and the sheet reloads the team's copy.
- **The whiteboard** keeps your strokes too, and merges them with the team's when you reconnect.
- **Everything else** (the board, chats, notes and guesses) needs a connection to save. If only WOW's live connection is down but your internet works, your own changes still save; teammates' changes appear once you're back.

In a sheet, the save status at the top right always tells you where you stand: **Connecting…**, **Saving…**, **Saved** or **Offline**.

If the notice doesn't go away, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting).

## Several tabs and devices

You can have WOW open in several tabs, or on your laptop and phone at once. Everything stays in step across them, including which notifications you've read.

Voice is the exception that needs a rule: you can only be unmuted in one room at a time. If you unmute in another tab or on another device, WOW mutes you in the first one, so your team never hears you twice.

<Callout type="phone">
  A phone makes a good second screen: keep the call or a puzzle's chat open on it while you work in
  the sheet on your laptop. See [WOW on your phone](/docs/guides/phone).
</Callout>

## How your time is counted

Each puzzle page has a timer chip with your time on that puzzle. It only counts while:

- the puzzle's tab is the one in front of you, and the window has focus, and
- you're active: after 5 minutes without moving the mouse, typing, scrolling or touching the page, it pauses. In the sheet it waits 30 minutes, since sheet work doesn't always look like activity to the page.

So a tab left open in the background, or overnight, doesn't add time. Your time feeds [your stats](/docs/guides/your-stats), the time-based awards on the [leaderboard](/docs/guides/leaderboard), and whether WOW offers you an **I helped** button when the puzzle is solved.
